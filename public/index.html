Key Stylometric Misinformation Signatures:

  • Headline Exclamations: Fake news articles average 0.315 exclamation marks per title, compared to 0.000 in professional wire copy.
  • Headline ALL-CAPS: Sensational articles employ ~20% uppercase letters for emotional agitation ("BOMBSHELL", "SHOCKING").
  • Publisher Dateline De-biasing: Agency tags (e.g. (Reuters) -) were stripped in preprocessing to ensure the model generalizes across news outlets.
